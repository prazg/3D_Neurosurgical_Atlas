3D Controls & Study Quick-Guide

Interactive Dissection, Stereotaxy & Active-Recall Tools

1 · Orbit, Pan & Depth Drill

  • Drag orbit · Right-drag or Shift+drag pan · Scroll zoom
  • Double-click structure to smoothly fly camera to target
  • Click the same spot repeatedly to cycle deeper through translucent shells (Layer 1 → N)
  • Click R L A P S I compass poles to align views

2 · Virtual Peel & X-Ray

  • H peel (hide) obstructing bone, cortex or tumour compartments; Shift+H restore all
  • I isolate selected structure · R reset camera framing
  • X toggle X-Ray translucency to highlight deep vessels, ventricles, tracts & lesions
  • L toggle priority-placed 3D anatomical pin labels

3 · 3D Caliper & 2D Section

  • M 3D surgical caliper: click two points for Euclidean mm + ΔR/L · ΔA/P · ΔS/I offsets
  • Sag Cor Ax plane clipping with live radiological 2 2D section view
  • Zoom, pan & click contours inside the 2D slice panel to cross-select in 3D
  • + .glb load custom local 3D Slicer / Blender meshes offline

4 · Walkthroughs, Quiz & Stereo

  • / instant structure & approach search · ? shortcut sheet
  • ← → step progressive craniotomy reveals & 102 Rhoton cadaveric plates
  • Quiz active-recall self-test (Find-it & Name-it with side-aware hints)
  • 3D Dubois red–cyan anaglyph stereo, VR WebXR & PNG snapshot export

Educational use only. A teaching aid built from open, de-identified datasets. Not a medical device, not patient-specific, and not for diagnosis, operative planning or navigation. Craniotomy footprints, stereotactic coordinates and entry points are schematic approximations.

Built from open anatomical and imaging collections — BodyParts3D, the SPL atlases, HCPex, HCP-1065, JHU, Jülich, VENAT 7T veins, CIT168, Harvard AAN, SUIT, OpenEar temporal bone, PAM50 spinal cord, TopCoW, BraDiPho left & right fibre dissections, TCIA/BraTS/PROTEAS/RSNA pathology cohorts, Servier Medical Art illustrations and Rhoton Collection plates (courtesy of the Rhoton Collection, American Association of Neurological Surgeons (AANS)/Neurosurgical Research and Education Foundation (NREF)) — each under its own terms.
