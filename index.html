Advanced 3D Surgical
Anatomy Atlas — Brain & Spine

An interactive 3D atlas of craniospinal anatomy and neuro-oncological pathology. Structures are clickable and labelled with anatomy notes, operative approaches and pitfalls, with captioned reference illustrations.

Anatomy

Craniospinal specimen, 327-structure MRI brain, head & neck CT with craniotomy footprints.

Overlay

Co-registered HCP-1065 tractography, arterial territories, HCP-MMP parcellation and cytoarchitecture.

Approaches

28 guided cranial, skull-base, functional and spinal approaches with step-by-step cards.

Pathology

Glioma by location and by WHO 2021 molecular class, vestibular schwannoma, spinal metastasis.

Educational use only. A teaching aid built from open, de-identified datasets. Not a medical device, not patient-specific, and not for diagnosis, operative planning or navigation. Craniotomy footprints and entry points are schematic approximations.

Built from open anatomical and imaging collections — BodyParts3D, the SPL atlases, HCPex, HCP-1065, JHU, the Jülich atlas, an MRA artery atlas, TCIA cohorts and Servier Medical Art illustrations — each under its own licence. Full attribution is in the atlas's Credits & licence panel and in the repository.
