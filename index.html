Brain & Spine

An interactive 3D atlas of craniospinal anatomy and neuro-oncological pathology. Structures are clickable and labelled with anatomy notes, operative approaches and pitfalls.

Open the atlas

Anatomy

Craniospinal specimen, 328-structure MRI brain, head & neck CT with craniotomy footprints.

Overlay

Co-registered white-matter tracts, arterial territories, HCP-MMP parcellation and cytoarchitecture.

Pathology

Glioma by location and by WHO 2021 molecular class, vestibular schwannoma, spinal metastasis.

Educational use only. A teaching aid built from open, de-identified datasets. Not a medical device, not patient-specific, and not for diagnosis, operative planning or navigation. Craniotomy footprints and entry points are schematic approximations.

Built from open anatomical and imaging collections — BodyParts3D, the SPL atlases, HCPex, JHU, the Jülich atlas, and TCIA cohorts — each under its own licence. Full attribution is in the atlas's Credits & licence panel and in the repository.
